Njeri of the Woods

Mutiny of the Consonants

I love my white friends, my awkward sweet crew With their sunburns and sandals, and ‘ I brought hummus too!’ They hug like puppies and speak with such glee While English sits heavy, just bullying me. They say ‘literally’ twenty seven times, They rhyme ‘drama’ with ‘llama’ call it good rhymes They chatter like teapots left boiling too long. And I'm nodding along, but my brain’s doing wrong. They mean well- bless their cotton socks But English feels like fighting ten angry rocks My tongue wants to jump ship,vowels want sleep My consonants mutiny, overboard they leap. One day they'll learn, one day they'll see That speaking English all day just finishes me.
